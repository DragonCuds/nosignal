# Demo assets and licences

All 30 instances are original, deliberately simple Lottie animations made for this package. Their editable definitions are `assets/build-assets.mjs`; its output is the three `.tgs` files beside it. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No Telegram asset or third-party artwork is included.
